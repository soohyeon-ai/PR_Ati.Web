TIDAL

Wafer Inspection and measurement system for semiconductor manufacturing.

High resolution & rapid wafer color inspection system

  • High-speed Colorful Inspection for Next-generation HBM4/4E
  • High Quality TDI Image (Mono/Color)
  • High Accuracy Stage, Real-time Auto Focus
  • Vision Review System
  • Bright Field & Dark Field Inspection
  • Multi Field Illumination
  • Capable of Full-wafer Inspection

Product Features

Front

Inspection : Die to die & Surface; Bright & Dark Field; IR Inspection (Void, Burnt). Measurement: Bump Height; Chip Warpage; IR Measurement (Accuracy). Application: Whole Wafer Image; ADC (Binning/Classification); RAC (Recipe Auto Create); Result Review System.

Edge

Inspection : Crack, Scratch, Chipping; Discoloration, Foreign Materials. Measurement: PEL (Process End Line); Profile, Depth, Width. Application: ADC (Binning/Classification).

Backside

Inspection : Color Inspection (3-Channel); Relative Algorithm. Application: ADC (Binning/Classification); Auto Focus & Illumination.
